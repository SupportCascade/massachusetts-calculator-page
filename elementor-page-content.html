What it includes Text Editor
<h2>What the Mortgage Calculator for Massachusetts Includes</h2>
<p>Most national calculators stop at principal and interest. Massachusetts buyers need the full picture, so this one adds the costs that actually show up on your monthly statement.</p>
<h3>Principal and Interest</h3>
<p>This is the core loan payment. Principal pays down what you borrowed, and interest is what the lender charges. Early in a 30-year loan, most of each payment goes to interest.</p>
<h3>Property Taxes in Canton, Quincy, Braintree and Nearby Towns</h3>
<p>Massachusetts towns set their own residential tax rates, and the spread is wide. Two homes at the same price in neighboring towns can carry very different tax bills. Most lenders collect taxes monthly through escrow, so they belong in your payment estimate.</p>
<h3>Homeowners Insurance</h3>
<p>Lenders require homeowners insurance. Coastal South Shore homes and older housing stock can cost more to insure, so get a real quote before you make an offer.</p>
<h3>PMI on Less Than 20% Down</h3>
<p>On a conventional loan with less than 20% down, lenders typically add private mortgage insurance. It can usually be removed once you build enough equity. FHA loans carry their own mortgage insurance with different rules.</p>
<h3>Condo and HOA Fees</h3>
<p>Condo fees are not part of your mortgage, but lenders count them when deciding what you can afford. For Quincy and Braintree condo buyers, the monthly fee can change your budget as much as the rate does.</p>

Mortgage options Text Editor
<h2>Mortgage Options to Compare in the Massachusetts Mortgage Calculator</h2>
<p>The right loan type changes your down payment, mortgage insurance and monthly cost. Run each option through the calculator, then confirm the details with a loan officer.</p>
<h3>Conventional Mortgage in Massachusetts</h3>
<p>Conventional loans are the most common mortgage in Massachusetts. They work well for buyers with solid credit, and PMI can be removed once you reach enough equity.</p>
<h3>FHA and HUD Lending</h3>
<p>FHA loans are insured through HUD and allow lower down payments and more flexible credit guidelines. They are popular with first-time buyers. Condos must be FHA approved, so check the building before you fall for a unit. William Raveis Mortgage is an FHA-approved lender.</p>
<h3>VA Loans</h3>
<p>Eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses can buy with no down payment and no monthly PMI.</p>
<h3>MassHousing and First-Time Buyer Programs</h3>
<p>Massachusetts offers programs for first-time and moderate-income buyers, including MassHousing loans with down payment assistance and Fannie Mae HomeReady. Eligibility depends on income, location and purchase price.</p>
<h3>Jumbo Loans</h3>
<p>Homes priced above the conforming loan limit need a jumbo loan. These usually require a larger down payment and stronger credit.</p>

Closing costs Text Editor
<h2>Massachusetts Closing Costs the Mortgage Calculator Doesn't Show</h2>
<p>Your monthly payment is only part of the cost. Budget for closing costs on top of your down payment. Common Massachusetts buyer costs include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Lender fees and appraisal</li>
<li>Closing attorney fees (Massachusetts closings are handled by attorneys)</li>
<li>Lender's title insurance, plus optional owner's title insurance</li>
<li>Recording fees at the Registry of Deeds</li>
<li>Prepaid property taxes, insurance and interest to set up escrow</li>
<li>Home inspection, plus any specialty inspections</li>
</ul>
<p>Your loan officer will give you a Loan Estimate with exact figures after you apply.</p>

Mortgage Calculator Massachusetts FAQs
FAQ items Accordion: title / content
How accurate is a Massachusetts mortgage calculator?
It is a solid estimate when you use a real interest rate quote and the correct town tax rate. Your final payment depends on your loan terms, insurance quote and assessed value.

Does this mortgage calculator include Massachusetts property taxes?
Yes. Choose your town and the calculator loads its residential tax rate. Taxes vary a lot between towns, so always check the specific town.

How much do I need to make to buy a house in Quincy or Canton?
It depends on price, down payment and debts. Many lenders look for total housing costs around 28% to 31% of gross monthly income, with total debts under roughly 43% to 50% depending on the loan. Run your target price above, then talk to a loan officer about your situation.

Can I use an FHA loan to buy a condo in Massachusetts?
Yes, if the condo building is FHA approved. Ask your agent to check approval status before you make an offer.

How much is a down payment on a first home in Massachusetts?
Conventional loans can start at 3% down for eligible first-time buyers, and FHA at 3.5%. MassHousing and other programs may help with down payment costs.

Should I get pre-approved before talking to an agent?
You can do either first. Many buyers talk to Mikel's team early, then get pre-approved through William Raveis Mortgage so the search and the financing line up.
